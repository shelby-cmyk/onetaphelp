How It Works

How OneTapHelp works.

From pressing the button to help arriving — every step is handled by trained professionals in the United States.

1

Press the button

Wear it as a pendant, wristband, or carry the mobile unit. One press is all it takes — at home, in the garden, or anywhere on the go.

2

Talk to a real person

You're connected to a US-based monitoring agent in seconds. They already know who you are, where you are, and your medical history.

3

Help arrives

The agent dispatches the right help — paramedics, family, or a trusted neighbor — and stays on the line until you're safe.

What Happens After You Order

From order to protection in under a week.

A

You order

Online or by phone. Takes about 5 minutes. We collect your address, emergency contacts, and any medical notes.

B

We test & ship

Every device is fully tested and pre-programmed before shipping. USPS Priority Mail with tracking — usually 2-3 days.

C

You set it up

Plug in the base unit (or charge the mobile pendant). One call to activate. We'll walk you through it step by step.

D

You're protected

From the moment activation completes, you're covered 24/7. Test the system anytime by pressing the button.

The Monitoring Center

The team that answers when you need them.
