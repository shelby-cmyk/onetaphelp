This Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice explains how OneTapHelp collects, uses, shares, and protects “consumer health data” under state laws including the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA), Nevada SB 370, and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act. It supplements our main Privacy Policy.

1. What is consumer health data?

Consumer health data is information that identifies a consumer’s past, present, or future physical or mental health status. In our context, this includes information you provide so that we can deliver medical alert services, such as:

  • Medical conditions, medications, allergies
  • Mobility limitations, fall history, fall risk indicators
  • Emergency contacts and health-related instructions for first responders
  • Device-generated data: button activations, fall-detection events, location at the time of an alert (for mobile/GPS plans)
  • Audio captured during a monitoring center call

2. How we collect it

  • Directly from you when you order, set up service, or update your account
  • From the device during use (button presses, fall detection, location at the time of an alert only)
  • From people you authorize (emergency contacts, family members designated by you, healthcare providers)
  • From our monitoring center partner in connection with answering an alert

3. How we use it

We use your consumer health data only to:

  • Provide, monitor, and dispatch emergency response
  • Deliver, maintain, and improve our service to you
  • Communicate with you, your emergency contacts, and dispatched responders
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your consumer health data for advertising or marketing.

4. With whom we share it

  • Emergency response personnel — paramedics, fire, police, family members designated by you, as needed to respond to an alert
  • Our monitoring center partner — bound by confidentiality and HIPAA-aware contracts
  • Service providers — equipment, payment processing, customer support — bound by data-protection agreements
  • Government / legal — only when required by law or to protect health and safety

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share consumer health data with advertisers, social platforms, or data brokers.

5. Your rights

Depending on the state where you live, you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we collect, use, or share your consumer health data
  • Access the consumer health data we hold about you
  • Withdraw consent for our collection or sharing
  • Delete your consumer health data, subject to limited legal exceptions (such as ongoing emergency response and required record retention)

6. How to exercise your rights

You can submit a request by:

  • Email: privacy@onetaphelp.com
  • Phone: 1-800-TAP-HELP
  • Mail: OneTapHelp, [Mailing Address]

We will respond within 45 days. We may need to verify your identity before completing your request, especially for access or deletion requests, to make sure we’re responding to the right person.

7. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time. If you withdraw consent for collection, we may not be able to continue providing service to you, since some consumer health data is required to dispatch emergency help safely. We will explain the consequences before processing your withdrawal.

8. Authorized agents

You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We will require written confirmation that the agent is authorized, and we may verify the consumer’s identity directly.

9. Appeals

If we deny your request, you may appeal by emailing privacy@onetaphelp.com with the subject line “Health Data Appeal.” We will respond to appeals within 45 days. Washington residents may also contact the Washington Attorney General’s Office at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

10. Retention

We retain your consumer health data only as long as needed to provide service, comply with legal obligations (such as the retention of emergency call records typically required by industry standards for at least seven years), and resolve disputes.

11. Changes

We may update this notice periodically. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. Where required by law, we will request renewed consent.
