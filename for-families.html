For Adult Children

You shouldn’t have to carry this alone.

Caring for an aging parent — even from across the country — is one of the hardest things a family does. OneTapHelp exists to make this one piece of it easier. For them, and for you.

We see what you’re carrying.

According to AARP and industry research, roughly 6 in 10 medical alert systems are purchased by adult children for a parent. So we know how this feels. You want them to keep their independence. They don’t want to feel like they’re losing it. The conversation is hard. The research is exhausting. And underneath all of it, there’s the worry — the kind that wakes you at 3am wondering if they’re okay.

We built OneTapHelp with that 3am moment in mind. So the path is gentler:

  • A real person on the phone — someone who’s walked dozens of families through this conversation. No scripts, no pressure.
  • Honest pricing, written plainly — what you see is what you pay. No “qualifying,” no fine print, no hidden fees.

  • Built around you, the caregiver — you handle billing and changes; they wear the device. Both of you stay in the loop.
The Conversation

Talking about it, gently.

This is a hard conversation for most families. There’s no perfect way — only kinder ways. Here’s what tends to land softly.

Lead with their independence

Instead of “I’m worried about you,” try “This is what helps you stay in your own home, on your own terms.” The reframe matters. Most parents aren’t resisting safety — they’re resisting the feeling of becoming someone who needs help.

Share what it gives you

“I’d sleep a little better knowing you have it — just for me.” Many parents accept the device as a quiet kindness to their adult children, even when they wouldn’t accept it for themselves. That’s OK. Love can be the reason.

Sit with them while they look

Modern medical alerts don’t look like the ones they remember from television. The pendant is small, waterproof, and easy to wear. When you show them in person — without pressure — the picture in their head often softens.

Quietly in the Background

You can help without taking anything away.

One of the hardest parts of caregiving is staying close without making someone feel watched. We’ve designed our service so you can be a quiet presence — there if needed, invisible if not.

You handle the billing

Set up the account in your own name. Use your card. Mom or Dad never sees a bill or has to remember a password.

You can be the first call

For non-life-threatening alerts, the monitoring center can reach you before anyone else — so you can decide together what happens next.

Gentle check-ins

Optional notifications when the device is tested, the battery is low, or anything needs attention. Nothing alarming — just enough to know things are working.

We talk to you

Account changes, address updates, tech questions — you call us, not them. We keep your loved one focused on living.
