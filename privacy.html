This Privacy Policy explains how OneTapHelp ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our medical alert services and visit our website at onetaphelp.com.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name, address, phone number, email
  • Date of birth, emergency contacts
  • Payment information (processed by our payment provider, not stored by us)
  • Medical conditions, allergies, and other health information you provide to ensure safe emergency response

Information you provide for connected-care features

  • Medication names, dosages, and schedules you enter to receive reminders
  • Appointment dates and details you enter to receive reminders
  • Wellness check-in responses and preferences
  • Family and caregiver contacts you authorize to receive alerts and updates

Information collected automatically

  • Device data: model, signal status, battery level, button activations, and fall-detection events
  • Location data (for mobile/GPS plans): we use location to enable emergency dispatch and, where you have enabled it, location-aware features. You can ask us about the location settings on your specific device.
  • Wellness-engagement activity: whether and how you respond to check-ins and reminders
  • Website usage: IP address, browser, pages visited, cookies (see Cookies section)

Information from third parties

We may receive information from emergency contacts, healthcare providers, family members and caregivers you designate, and our monitoring center partners.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, monitor, and dispatch emergency response services
  • Deliver connected-care features you turn on — medication and appointment reminders, wellness check-ins, and family/caregiver alerts
  • Process payments, manage your subscription, and send service-related communications
  • Communicate with you about your account, including support, billing, and service notices
  • Send marketing communications (you may opt out at any time)
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

3. Automated Technology & AI

Some features use automated technology, including AI, to support your care experience — for example, scheduling and delivering reminders, prompting wellness check-ins, and routing communications to you and your designated contacts.

Our automated and AI features are support tools, not medical advice or emergency dispatch. They do not diagnose, treat, or make clinical decisions, and they are not a substitute for professional medical care or for calling 911 in an emergency. A live emergency always reaches a human operator.

We do not use automated processing to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you without a human involved. We do not sell your information, and we do not use your health information to train advertising models.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only as follows:

  • Emergency response: With monitoring center operators, dispatch personnel, paramedics, and emergency contacts you designate, as needed to respond to an alert.
  • Family & caregivers you authorize: When you enable family alerts, we share alert notifications and the wellness or status updates you choose with the family members and caregivers you designate. You control who is on this list and can add or remove people at any time. The account holder (or their authorized representative) decides what these contacts can see.
  • Service providers: With vendors who help us operate our service (equipment, monitoring center, payment processing, IT, customer support, and the technology that powers reminders and communications). These vendors are contractually required to protect your information.
  • Legal: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your health information for advertising purposes.

5. Health Information & State Privacy Laws

Some of the information you provide is "consumer health data" under state laws including the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA), Nevada SB 370, and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act. We collect, use, and share consumer health data only to provide our services and as described in this Policy.

You have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we collect your consumer health data
  • Access your consumer health data
  • Withdraw consent for collection or sharing
  • Have your consumer health data deleted

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@onetaphelp.com.

6. Communications & TCPA Consent

By providing your phone number and submitting our forms, you consent to receive calls and text messages from OneTapHelp — including calls placed using automated telephone dialing systems and pre-recorded messages — for service-related purposes (emergency response, account, billing, reminders, and wellness check-ins) and, if you opt in, marketing.

We may communicate with you and your designated contacts across multiple channels — voice, SMS text, email, and in-app messaging — depending on the features you enable. SMS programs are operated in compliance with carrier requirements (including A2P 10DLC registration); email marketing follows CAN-SPAM. Consent is not a condition of purchasing our services. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by replying STOP to a text or following the unsubscribe instructions in an email. Note that opting out of marketing does not stop essential service and emergency communications.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies on our website. You control which categories you allow through our Cookie Preferences. You can also see a full inventory on our Cookie Policy page.

Categories

  • Essential — required for the site to function (UTM source attribution for routing your call, dynamic phone-number display, form submission, security). Cannot be turned off.
  • Analytics — helps us understand which pages are useful and where to improve. Examples: Google Analytics 4, internal pageview events. Off until you opt in.
  • Marketing — lets us show relevant ads on other sites and measure those campaigns. Examples: Meta Pixel, Google Ads conversion tracking. Off until you opt in.

Browser signals we honor

  • Do Not Track (DNT): if your browser sends a DNT signal, we automatically reject all non-essential tracking without showing the consent banner.
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC): we treat GPC signals as a request to opt out of the “sale” or “share” of personal information under California, Colorado, Connecticut, and similar state laws.

Changing your preferences

Click Cookie Preferences in any page footer to revisit your choices, or visit our Cookie Policy for the full list.

8. Your Rights (CCPA, CPRA, GDPR, and state laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information (subject to legal exceptions)
  • Opt out of certain sharing or processing
  • Receive a portable copy of your information
  • Not face discrimination for exercising these rights

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@onetaphelp.com or call 1-800-TAP-HELP.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal information as long as your account is active and as required to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Emergency call records are retained for at least seven years as required by industry standards.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information, including encryption in transit and at rest. No system is 100% secure, but we work to reduce risk.

11. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date and, where required, notify you directly.
