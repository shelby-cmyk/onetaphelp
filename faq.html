Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Don't see your question? Send us a message or call 1-800-TAP-HELP.

How fast does someone respond when I press the button?

Our monitoring center averages a response of under 20 seconds from button-press to a live US-based agent on the line. The agent already has your information, address, and emergency contacts — they don't need you to repeat anything if you can't speak.

Do I need a phone line to use OneTapHelp?

No. Both OneTapHelp Pendant and Wristband include built-in cellular through AT&T or T-Mobile — no landline required. Works anywhere with cell coverage, in or out of the home.

What happens if I press the button by accident?

That's totally fine and very common — especially in the first few weeks. Just tell the agent it was accidental and they'll cancel the alert. No charge, no hassle, no judgment. Better to test it than to wonder if it works.

How does fall detection work?

The OneTapHelp Go pendant uses motion sensors to automatically detect a fall. If a fall is detected, the device contacts the monitoring center automatically, even if you can't press the button. No fall detection system is 100% accurate, so we recommend pressing the button manually whenever possible.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All OneTapHelp plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime — no penalties, no termination fees. We earn your business every month.

What is the 30-day guarantee?

If you're not satisfied within 30 days of activation, return the equipment and we'll refund your $49.95 setup fee in full. You'll still pay for any service days used, prorated.

Who answers when I press the button?

A live US-based trained, certified operator at our TMA Five Diamond Certified, UL-Listed monitoring center. Every operator is trained in emergency dispatch, mental health awareness, and senior care. Calls are never routed offshore. The same infrastructure protects over 1 million seniors nationwide.

Is the pendant waterproof? Can I shower with it?

Yes. All OneTapHelp pendants are waterproof and designed to be worn in the shower or bath — where most slips and falls happen. Wear it 24/7 for full protection.

How long does the battery last?

The OneTapHelp Pendant has 18+ days of battery life on a single charge — among the longest in the industry. The Wristband lasts about 7 days per charge. Both use simple magnetic charging docks.

Does Medicare or insurance cover this?

Standard Medicare does not cover medical alert systems. Some Medicare Advantage plans, long-term care insurance plans, and Veterans Affairs benefits may cover all or part of the cost. We can provide a paid invoice you can submit to your insurance for reimbursement.

What if I move?

Just let us know your new address. The system works anywhere in the United States with cell coverage. No moving fee, no service interruption.

How do I cancel?

Call us at 1-800-TAP-HELP or email help@onetaphelp.com. We'll send you a prepaid return label. Once we receive the equipment back, your service ends and any unused billing period is prorated.

Still have questions?

Call us. A real person will answer in under a minute.
